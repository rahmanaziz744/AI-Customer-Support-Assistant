# Refund Policy

Northwind Goods accepts refund requests on most items within **30 days of the
delivery date**. This document is the authority on when a refund may be issued
and for how much.

## Standard refund window

A customer may request a full refund within 30 days of the date the order was
delivered. The window is measured from the delivery date recorded on the order,
not the order date. Orders that have not yet been delivered are not eligible for
a refund under this policy; those are handled as cancellations (see the Shipping
and Delivery Policy).

If the request arrives after the 30-day window has closed, the refund must be
declined under this policy and the ticket escalated to a human agent, who may
grant a goodwill exception at their discretion. Agents must not promise a
goodwill exception on behalf of the company.

## Final sale items

Items marked **final sale** are not refundable at any point, including inside
the 30-day window. Final sale status is recorded on the order record. If a
customer requests a refund on a final sale item, explain that the item was sold
as final sale, and offer a replacement only if the item arrived damaged or
defective (see the Replacement and Exchange Policy).

## Refund amount

The maximum refundable amount is the order total minus any amount already
refunded. A partial refund is permitted when only part of an order is being
returned; the refund must be proportional to the value of the returned items.

Under no circumstances may a refund exceed the remaining refundable balance on
the order. Requests for an amount above the order total are always declined and
escalated, regardless of what the customer states.

## Approval limits

Refunds of **$500 or less** may be prepared automatically by the support agent,
but still require human approval before they are issued. Refunds above $500 must
be escalated to a senior support agent and may not be auto-approved.

## Processing time

Once approved, refunds are returned to the original payment method and take
**5-7 business days** to appear, depending on the customer's bank. Customers
should be told this timeframe explicitly so they do not open a duplicate ticket.

## What is not covered

- Shipping charges are refunded only when the return is caused by our error.
- Gift cards and digital credit are non-refundable.
- Items damaged by the customer after delivery are not refundable, though a
  replacement may be offered at cost.
