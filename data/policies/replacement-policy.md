# Replacement and Exchange Policy

A replacement sends the customer the same item again at no charge. An exchange
sends a different variant (size, colour) of the same product. Both are covered
here.

## Replacement window

Replacements may be issued within **45 days of delivery** when the item is
defective, damaged, or materially different from what was ordered. This window
is deliberately longer than the 30-day refund window because manufacturing
defects often surface only after some use.

## Damaged on arrival

If an item arrives visibly damaged, the customer should report it within
**7 days of delivery**. Damage reported inside this window is replaced without
requiring the customer to return the damaged item; ask them to photograph it and
dispose of it. Damage reported after 7 days but within the 45-day window still
qualifies for a replacement, but the original item must be returned first.

## Final sale items

Final sale items cannot be refunded, but they **can** be replaced if they
arrived damaged or defective. This is the one exception to the final sale rule.
A final sale item that the customer simply does not want is neither refundable
nor replaceable.

## Limits

Only **one replacement per order** may be issued automatically. A customer
requesting a second replacement on the same order must be escalated to a human
agent, as repeated replacement requests may indicate a fulfilment problem or
abuse that needs investigation.

## Out of stock

If the replacement item is out of stock, do not promise a shipping date. Offer
the customer a choice between a full refund (subject to the Refund Policy) and
waiting for restock, and escalate so a human can confirm restock timing.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or colour follow the same 45-day window. If the
new variant costs more, the customer pays the difference; if it costs less, the
difference is refunded to the original payment method.
