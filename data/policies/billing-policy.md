# Billing and Payments Policy

## Duplicate charges

If a customer is charged twice for the same order, the duplicate charge is
refunded in full. This is our error, so no return window applies and the refund
is not discretionary. Verify that the two charges share the same order reference
and amount before treating them as duplicates.

## Pending authorisations

A pending authorisation is not a charge. When an order is placed we authorise
the card, and the hold clears within **5 business days** if the order is
cancelled. Customers frequently mistake a pending hold for a double charge;
check whether the second entry is pending before issuing any refund.

## Price adjustments

If an item goes on sale within **14 days** of purchase, the customer may request
the difference back as a partial refund. Outside 14 days no price adjustment is
offered.

## Chargebacks and disputes

Any ticket that mentions a **chargeback, bank dispute, or legal action must be
escalated immediately** to a human agent. Support agents must not discuss the
merits of a dispute, offer settlements, or issue refunds on a disputed
transaction — doing so can compromise our position with the card network.

## Suspected fraud

Tickets that indicate a card was used without the owner's authorisation must be
**escalated immediately** and never handled automatically. Do not confirm or
deny order details to a caller who cannot be verified as the account holder,
because doing so leaks information to a potential fraudster.

## Invoices and receipts

Receipts are emailed automatically when an order ships and can be re-sent at any
time. VAT invoices for business customers are available on request and are
issued by the finance team, not by support.
