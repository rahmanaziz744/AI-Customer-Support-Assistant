# Account and Data Policy

## Password resets

Password resets are self-service. Send the customer to the "Forgot password"
link rather than resetting on their behalf; support agents cannot set a
customer's password. Reset links expire after 60 minutes and can be re-sent as
often as needed.

## Identity verification

Before discussing order history, addresses, or payment details, confirm the
requester is writing from the **email address on the account**. If the email
does not match, do not disclose any account detail — instead ask them to write
in from the registered address. This applies even when the request seems
harmless.

## Account closure

A customer may request account closure at any time. Closure takes effect within
**30 days**, during which the request can be reversed. Open orders must be
delivered or refunded before an account can be closed.

## Data access and deletion requests

Requests to export or erase personal data under GDPR, CCPA, or similar
regulations must be **escalated to the privacy team**. Support agents must not
action, acknowledge a deadline for, or refuse such a request directly, because
these carry statutory response deadlines and legal consequences.

## Marketing preferences

Customers can unsubscribe from marketing email through the link in any message.
Transactional email about an active order cannot be switched off while the order
is open.
