# Warranty and Technical Support Policy

## Coverage

All electronics carry a **12-month manufacturer warranty** from the delivery
date, covering defects in materials and workmanship. Customers who purchased the
extended plan are covered for **24 months**. Proof of purchase is required; the
order record in our system satisfies this.

The warranty does **not** cover accidental damage, liquid damage, normal wear,
cosmetic marks, or damage from unauthorised repair. Those requests are declined
under warranty, though a paid repair may be offered.

## Troubleshooting first

Before authorising a repair or replacement, walk the customer through the basic
troubleshooting steps for the product category. A large share of reported faults
are resolved by a firmware update or a factory reset. Document which steps the
customer has already tried so a human agent does not repeat them.

## Repairs

Warranty repairs take **10-14 days** from the date we receive the item. We pay
shipping in both directions for in-warranty repairs. If the item cannot be
repaired, it is replaced with an equivalent model.

## Overlap with the refund window

A fault reported inside the 30-day refund window is handled as a refund or
replacement at the customer's choice, not as a warranty repair — it is faster
and the customer has the stronger right. Only use the warranty path once the
refund and replacement windows have closed.

## Escalation

Escalate to a human agent when the customer reports a **safety issue** —
overheating, smoke, electric shock, or battery swelling. These require incident
logging and possible recall action, and must never be closed automatically.
