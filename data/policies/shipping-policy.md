# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Delivery estimates

Standard shipping arrives in **3-5 business days**. Express shipping arrives in
**1-2 business days**. Estimates exclude weekends and public holidays, and begin
when the order ships, not when it is placed. Orders placed after 2pm local time
ship the following business day.

Shipping is free on orders over **$60**; below that a flat $6.95 fee applies.

## Delayed orders

An order is considered delayed once it passes its estimated delivery date. For a
delayed order, confirm the tracking number and current carrier status, then set
expectations honestly. Do not invent a new delivery date — quote only what the
carrier reports.

If the order is more than **3 business days** past its estimate, offer the
customer the choice of waiting or cancelling for a full refund.

## Lost packages

A package is declared lost once tracking has shown no movement for
**10 consecutive days**. At that point the customer is entitled to either a
replacement or a full refund, at their choice, and shipping charges are refunded
because the failure is ours.

Before declaring a package lost, confirm the shipping address on the order is
correct and ask the customer to check with neighbours and their building's
mail room. Carriers frequently mark packages delivered a day before they arrive.

## Wrong address

If the customer supplied an incorrect address and the package was delivered
there, we are not obliged to refund or replace, though a goodwill replacement at
cost may be offered. If we shipped to the wrong address through our own error,
issue a replacement immediately at no charge.

## Cancellations

An order may be cancelled for a full refund at any time **before it ships**.
Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled; it must be returned
under the Refund Policy instead.

## International shipping

International orders may be held by customs, which is outside our control. Duties
and import taxes are the customer's responsibility and are not refundable.
